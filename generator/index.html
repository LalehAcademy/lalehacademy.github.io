Certificate images are exported as high-resolution PNGs, not PDFs — reliable in-browser PDF generation would need an extra library and offers no real benefit for image-based certificates. PNGs at this resolution (3300×2550, ~300 DPI) print cleanly; convert to PDF afterward with any print/export tool if you specifically need that format.
